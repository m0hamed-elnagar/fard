# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 14, 2026

Mohamed Elnagar ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Fard (Qada Tracker)** mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your spiritual and personal data is handled in a safe, transparent, and responsible manner.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed as an **offline-first** application. Most of your data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

### A. Personal Data
* **No Account Creation / Registration:** You do not need to register, create an account, or provide any personal details (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.
* **Spiritual & Tracking Data:** All records of your missed prayers (Qada), Quran reading progress (Werd), bookmarks, Tasbih counts, and custom Azkar are stored **locally** on your device using a local database (Hive NoSQL). We do not have access to, nor do we collect, this data.

### B. Device Permissions & On-Device Data
To provide its features, the App requests access to certain device capabilities:
* **Location Data:** The App requests location permissions (Approximate or Precise Location) to calculate accurate Islamic prayer times and determine the Qibla direction based on your coordinates.
  * **On-Device Calculation:** Location coordinates are processed strictly **on-device** using local calculation libraries and are **never** uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.
* **Notifications:** The App requests permission to send notifications to remind you of prayer times and morning/evening Azkar.
* **Audio & Background Execution:** The App uses audio and background services to play Quran recitations while your screen is off or the App is in the background.
* **Vibration:** The App uses device haptics/vibration to simulate physical beads for the Tasbih counter feature.
* **Internet Access:** The App requests internet permission to:
  1. Fetch Quran text, translations, and Mushaf page images.
  2. Download Quran audio recitations for offline playback.
  * **No Tracking:** These requests are standard asset downloads; no personal data or tracking identifiers are sent during these downloads.

### C. Ad-Free Policy
* **No Advertisements:** The App is completely ad-free. We do not integrate any advertising SDKs and do not share any data with marketing or advertising networks.

### D. Third-Party Analytics and Performance Services
To help us monitor performance, identify bugs, and improve the user experience, we use trusted, anonymous telemetry and diagnostic services:
* **Firebase Analytics (Google LLC):** Collects anonymous usage metrics (such as active users, screen views, and button interactions) to help us understand how the App is used.
* **Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC):** Collects anonymous crash logs and system telemetry when the App encounters an unexpected error or crash.
* **Firebase Performance Monitoring (Google LLC):** Measures App latency, loading times, and network performance anonymously.

These services may gather basic device telemetry (e.g., device model, operating system version, and region). Please refer to Google's privacy policies for details:
* [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 2. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Local Data:** All local data (Qada logs, Werd progress, custom settings) remains on your device until you delete the App or clear the App's storage cache. You can also export or import your data manually using the backup tools in the App's settings.
* **Telemetry Data:** Anonymous analytics and crash logs collected by Firebase are retained in accordance with Google Firebase policies and are used solely for diagnostic and improvement purposes.

## 3. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take the necessary actions.
